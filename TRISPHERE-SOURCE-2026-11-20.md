*For Matthew Chenoweth Wright (Monolithic LLC) and Millie, from A. Beth Jones's Universal 13-Layer Tri-Sphere Blueprint. Prepared 19 November 2026.*

**Purpose.** Matthew's "Energy Through the Hourglass" built the return-loop kernel: a diverted-tokamak analogue with separate particle and energy balances. This specification lays out the full TriSphere architecture so a prototype simulator models Beth's structure, not only the tokamak slice. The tokamak kernel remains the testable core; everything below extends it to the whole framework. Honest labels are carried throughout: established geometry and physics, interpretive assembly, speculative mechanism.

## 1. Structure

- **13 layers, 0–12.** Layer 0 = CRP (centre, empty mirror). Layer 6 = Filter. Layer 7 = Product (dodecahedron). Layer 12 = asymptotic shell (the epidermis). Boundary condition: v(12) = 0 — the shell contains; flow cannot cross it.
- **Layers are energetic flow patterns, not static boxes.** They breathe in and out. Nothing sits still.
- **Mirror map M(n) = 12 − n.** Pairs: 0↔12, 1↔11, 2↔10, 3↔9, 4↔8, 5↔7; Layer 6 maps to itself. Byproducts rest on the shelf of their mirrored layer (8 rests at 4, 9 rests at 3).
- **One toroidal field** for the entire architecture; the layers sit inside it and it grows with the layer. Three aspects within the one field: Time (clockwise), Phase (counterclockwise), Space (static / fluctuation zone).
- **Skeleton vs scaffolding.** Hourglass + toroidal field = the energetic skeleton (consciousness / circulation). Geometric scaffolding (64-node tetrahedron at Layer 6, Merkaba at Layer 12) = the body / form. The circulation is one flow through all the shapes.

## 2. Material

- **One substance: LCL (liquid crystal light).** The entire structure is LCL at various geometrical and energetic states. Density varies by coordinate — same substance, different amounts. Light is a byproduct of LCL (Source reflecting off self), not the base material. (When Beth says "plasma," she means LCL.)

## 3. Flow and loop-back

- **One continuous string, no ends.** Out 0→12, around the boundary inside Layer 11, back through the lattice to the CRP. 0 and 12 are mirror opposites (a mirror relation, not an identification).
- **The breath.** The CRP pushes forward; the shell pushes back. This is the circulation.
- **Layer 11 is the smooth slope** (spherical / toroidal), feeding back at the southern ports. Layers 10 (hourglass) and 11 (toroidal field) create the rise and fall around the 11th boundary; all shelves are inside that structure.
- **Seams are elastic.** That elasticity is how Layer 12 constricts in on itself and becomes the CRP again, pushing everything inside. Layer 12 is the outside; everything breathing is inside Layer 12.
- **CRP / shell.** Even as a singularity the CRP has an asymptotic shell, compressed to the point (not collapsed or gone). 0 represents the shell and the empty space combined. There is no true singularity.

## 4. Equation of motion (tenth equation)

ρ'' = −V'(ρ) − γρ' + D(ρ, k) — a driven, damped oscillator. ρ is the layer coordinate and, in Beth's framework, the observer's perspective (see §7).

- **V(ρ)** rises, then flattens inside Layer 11 and runs flat to the wall (the flat band). Three resting shelves: near 4, near 8, near 11–12. **V is an effective potential, chosen not derived** — implement the shelves as effective-potential features arising from elastic seams, not as gravitational wells (a smooth gravitational potential is monotone and cannot hold shelves).
- **γ** = damping / decay. Allows storage and reuse of byproducts; breaks time-translation symmetry (the Noether point: a conservative potential alone cannot store and reuse).
- **D(ρ, k)** = drive. At the CRP, D is the avatar's intent — the system is not self-contained; the avatar pumps it. **Implement D as a powered / time-dependent drive**: a constant autonomous D with constant γ > 0 cannot sustain a limit cycle.
- **k** = valve opening, set by intent. Wide = stable limit cycle; closing = decay toward heat death (a bifurcation).
- **Target behaviour:** a stable limit cycle (the breath), with shelves as conveyor belts (see §6).

## 5. The Filter (Layer 6)

- **Intent splits the flow:** Product (→ Layer 7) or Compost (→ Layers 8–11).
- **Two dials:** σ(t) sets the Product vs Compost ratio; c_i sets the byproduct distribution across layers.
- **The human sits at the Filter.** The centre (0) is empty and must stay empty — the avatar is not at the centre.
- **Symmetry is broken here by a decision.** Avatar intent pushes the flow forward or backward. Choice is energy; the perspective is the choice.

## 6. Shelves, valves, seams

- **Nodes = filter valves. Seams = transportation ports** between valves; seams outline and create the shape of the shelves. Seam count matches node count.
- **Shelves are conveyor belts, not parking shelves.** The valve constantly pushes and pulls whatever is on them: maintained and lifted up, or decaying and falling down.
- **Three states, no on/off point** in an infinite loop: repair, maintain, decay. Seam direction and count set the state — all seams one direction = maintain; seams pulling apart = decay; seams converging into one node = unification / repair. (The duality, two nodes, is a closed loop that only spins — the kinetic conflict of duality. The triangle, three nodes, can hold repair + maintain + decay at once.)
- **Release mechanism:** intent, full repurposing, repair from an avatar's interaction, or natural decay.
- **Every layer** is made of shelves, nodes, and tubules (tubules are the seams of the shelves); the three named in the tenth equation are the resting shelves.
- **The shelves are the result of the well:** seams and valves create both the flow of the well and the planes of the shelves — one mechanism in a discrete node-and-seam lattice.

## 7. The avatar and the observer

- **Recognition generates layers:** recognising the centre as a node → Layer 4; as north and south → Layer 6; self as Source → the 64-node tetrahedron. (Lower-level geometry, Layers 3–6, is still being worked out — do not force a resolution.)
- **Standing:** layer numbers are positions only; whether a shape breathes or traps is decided by standing, not number parity — ego renders the geometry flat and boxed, Source renders it true. The consciousness dimensions stand (3D hourglass at Layer 2, 5D diamond at Layer 4, 7D dodecahedron skybox at Layer 7). The avatar is the breath moving through the form; the nodes are the filter valves letting it breathe layer to layer. Unrecognised, the avatar stays trapped in the boxed rendering at the lower consciousness.
