Model boundaries

The 3D reactor is a generic schematic. Its circular plasma surfaces, coil positions, and targets are illustrative. The separate poloidal cross-section below shows the analytic X-point and separatrix. Tracers follow prescribed paths in normalized animation time. They do not model particle orbits, exhaust transport, heat load, or reactor performance. The radial oscillator is an illustrative closure of Jones’s Equation 10, with a specified center kick and a chosen harmonic potential.
