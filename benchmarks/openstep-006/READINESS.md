# What remains before a working reactor can be claimed

| Area | Evidence now available | Status | Concrete closure requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static equilibrium | OPENSTEP-001–004 fixed-source and fixed-operating-point solves; residual checks | Demonstrated numerical calculations | Independent solver/device comparison with matched profiles, current, geometry and conventions |
| Axisymmetric stability | Published DIII-D vessel/equilibrium benchmark; reproducible eigenvalue, two-grid check, resistivity scaling and time evolution | Reference benchmark partly verified; time/eigenvalue comparison fails | Explain and reproduce the linear/time discrepancy; import STEP passive structure thicknesses, resistivities, connectivity and circuit coupling; match a displacement measurement |
| Controller | Native DIII-D prescribed F9 differential current tests, saturation, slew and delay sweeps | Conditional short-transient response | Actual STEP L/R/mutual matrices, power-supply voltage/current limits, sensor transfer functions, latency and noise; robust full-transient control tests |
| General plasma stability | Existing q/beta diagnostics only | UNRESOLVED | Ballooning/kink/tearing and other relevant 3D MHD calculations with qualified plasma/wall/rotation models; disruption and runaway-electron assessment |
| Sustained burn and current | Unfitted thermal D-T reactivity reproduces source alpha power; 72 temperature-feedback energy cases | Conditional; central reduced model unstable | Coupled ion/electron energy, particle/helium-ash transport, radiation and alpha slowing down; heating/burn controller; noninductive current-drive and bootstrap sustainment |
| Exhaust and cooling | OPENSTEP-005 fixed-equilibrium heat mapping; 108 conduction/convection assumptions and radiation requirements | Conditional; no qualified target | SOL transport, neutrals and detachment calculation; target CAD and materials; coolant hydraulics, critical heat flux, erosion and transient loads |
| Mechanical and magnet integrity | Prior external coil forces and magnetic pressure screens | UNRESOLVED | Actual conductor, support and joint design; stress/fatigue, quench protection, cryogenic loads and irradiation limits |
| Neutronics and fuel | Source-equivalent burn rate, 135 breeding/recovery/loss cases, processing hold-up estimates | Arithmetic demonstrated; self-sufficiency UNRESOLVED | Blanket and shielding CAD/materials, neutron transport and activation, TBR uncertainty, measured extraction/recycling/retention and decay, start-up fuel supply |
| Net electricity and availability | 162 explicitly assumed electric-balance cases, including negative results | Conditional | Coupled validated heat conversion, current-drive efficiency, pumps/cryogenics/control/fuel-cycle loads and maintenance/reliability model |
| Physical device | No hardware built or operated in this work | NOT DEMONSTRATED | Reviewed engineering design, qualified components, facility and experimental commissioning evidence |

The next computational priority is closing the native linear/time validation discrepancy. The next external-input priority is a real passive-vessel/circuit/controller package for the selected STEP-like configuration. Until then, DIII-D reference success and hypothetical favorable plant settings cannot establish a working STEP reactor. No percentage-complete reactor score is assigned.
