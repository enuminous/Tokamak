Test a second implementation that assembles the driven heat equation directly, using backward Euler, an independent tridiagonal solve, and no analytic steady profile. Use 64 cells. Run all 32 cases, with the recorded heat input and both cooling schedules. Compare the front face just before removing the heat at 2 s and at the end of cooldown at 5 s with the same-side samples in the archived 009 history.
