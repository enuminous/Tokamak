# Component requirements and failure modes

The boundary is a historical STEP-like power plant at functional subsystem level, plus every executable Tokamak lab and benchmark family. This register defines testable allocations; an as-built parts list, P&IDs, hazard log, site configuration and vendor limits are required to descend to individual valves, channels, bolts, welds or lines of code. All physical acceptance gates below are UNRESOLVED. Each cell under “challenge” describes a test to conduct or commission; actual runs and their provenance are listed in the [verification report](README.md). Consequences describe credible mechanisms, without estimated probabilities. Protections are proposed verification targets. The integrated argument must include qualification of the protection itself.
